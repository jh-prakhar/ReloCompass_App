# Student Relocation Guide: Accommodation

## Finding Safe and Affordable Housing Abroad

Finding suitable accommodation is one of the most important steps when relocating abroad for studies. Here are the main accommodation types available to international students:

### Types of Student Accommodation

1. **Student Dormitories (On-Campus)**
   - Usually the most affordable option
   - Located on or near campus
   - Typically include utilities and internet
   - Limited privacy but excellent for meeting other students
   - Often managed by the university housing office

2. **Shared Apartments (Off-Campus)**
   - Rent an apartment with 1-3 other students
   - Each person has a bedroom; kitchen and living area are shared
   - More affordable than renting alone
   - Requires a lease agreement

3. **Studio Apartments**
   - Self-contained unit with bedroom, kitchen, and bathroom
   - More expensive but offers complete privacy
   - Good for students who prefer quiet study environments

4. **Homestays**
   - Live with a local family
   - Includes meals in some cases
   - Excellent for cultural immersion and language practice
   - Usually arranged through agencies or university programs

### Rental Tips for International Students

- **Start early**: Begin searching 2-3 months before your arrival
- **Verify the landlord**: Check reviews, ask for references, confirm ownership
- **Understand the lease**: Read every clause before signing. Look for hidden fees, deposit terms, and cancellation policies
- **Budget for upfront costs**: Most rentals require first month's rent + security deposit (1-2 months)
- **Check proximity to campus**: Factor in commute time and public transit costs
- **Take photos**: Document the property condition at move-in to protect your deposit

### Rental Scam Awareness

International students are frequent targets of rental scams. Watch for these red flags:

- **Requests for money before viewing**: Never send deposits before seeing the property in person or via video tour
- **Prices that are too good to be true**: Compare with average rents in the area
- **Pressure to act fast**: Scammers create urgency to prevent you from thinking critically
- **No verifiable listing**: Always cross-reference on multiple platforms
- **Requests for wire transfers or gift cards**: Legitimate landlords accept traceable payment methods
- **Grammar errors in communications**: Often a sign of fraudulent listings

### Cost of Living Considerations

When budgeting for accommodation, factor in:
- Monthly rent
- Utilities (electricity, water, gas, internet)
- Contents insurance (optional but recommended)
- Transportation from accommodation to campus
- Initial setup costs (furniture, kitchen supplies)

Typical student rent ranges by city (approximate, for reference only — always verify current rates):
- Toronto, Canada: CAD 750-1,500/month
- Berlin, Germany: EUR 400-800/month
- London, UK: GBP 800-1,500/month
- Sydney, Australia: AUD 600-1,200/month
- New York, USA: USD 1,200-2,500/month
- Amsterdam, Netherlands: EUR 600-1,200/month

Always verify current rental prices using local rental websites and university accommodation offices.
